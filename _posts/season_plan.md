## 1) Strategic Overview

- Periodization approach (Thorsson Method): Curved-load macrocycle with early specificity for the B race (run emphasis) while preserving triathlon frequency, followed by progressive aerobic base expansion, strength-endurance development, and an early race-specific build for the 70.3. Load is organized in 3:1 waves (three loading weeks, one deload).
- Anchors:
  - Immediate run-sharpen + taper to respect the B race.
  - Post-B race recovery/reset to clear fatigue and set the posture for development.
  - Two consecutive base blocks (frequency → capacity → muscular endurance) across all three disciplines.
  - Early 70.3-specific build that positions the athlete to enter a 14-day taper starting 2026-04-26 for the A race.
- Environmental alignment: Begin heat/humidity adaptation and fueling rehearsal in the early build to align with Danang’s climate.

## 3) Phase Details

### Phase 1 — Run Sharpen + Taper (B Race Focus)
Dates: 2025-11-08 → 2025-12-07 (race day)  
- Primary goals:
  - Convert existing endurance to run efficiency and pace control for half marathon.
  - Maintain swim/bike frequency with reduced load to preserve triathlon readiness.
- Weekly volume: 6–10 hours (gradual reduction over final 10–12 days).
- Intensity distribution: Pyramidal ~75/20/5 (Z1–2/Z3/Z4–5). Limit high-intensity to short, sharp touches.
- Key modalities/workouts:
  - Run: 1 long run (75–100 min) with HM-pace segments (e.g., 2–3×10–15 min) earlier in the phase; 1 quality session (threshold-cruise intervals or HM-10K pace work); strides.
  - Bike: 1–2 easy aerobic spins; optional short sweet spot maintenance early (e.g., 2×10–12 min).
  - Swim: 2–3 sessions emphasizing technique + short threshold set early; taper to aerobic + drills race week.
  - Strength: Light maintenance, mobility, foot/hip hygiene.
- Transition criteria (into Phase 2):
  - B race complete with normal post-race soreness only.
  - No escalating niggles; athlete tolerates easy aerobic sessions within 72 hours post-race.

Race-week note (B priority):
- Reduce run volume ~35–50% vs. prior peak week; retain 1 short HM-pace primer (e.g., 2–3×5–6 min) 4–5 days out.
- Keep swim/bike frequency low-load to aid recovery and maintain rhythm.

### Phase 2 — Post-B Recovery & Recalibration
Dates: 2025-12-08 → 2025-12-21 (2 weeks)  
- Primary goals:
  - Restore musculoskeletal freshness and autonomic balance.
  - Reintroduce strength training with controlled progression.
- Weekly volume: Week 1: 4–8 hours; Week 2: 6–10 hours.
- Intensity distribution: Polarized ~90/10 (nearly all easy).
- Key modalities/workouts:
  - Swim: Easy aerobic + drills; technique resets.
  - Bike: Short aerobic rides; optional neuromuscular spin-ups.
  - Run: Short, easy runs; reintroduce strides in week 2 if fully recovered.
  - Strength: Begin a 4–6 week progressive block (squat/hinge/push/pull/carry), 2×/week, low reps/high quality.
- Transition criteria (into Phase 3):
  - No residual soreness from B race; easy back-to-back days tolerated.
  - Able to perform basic strength patterns pain-free; technique remains stable under light load.

### Phase 3 — Aerobic Base I (Frequency & Technique)
Dates: 2025-12-22 → 2026-02-02 (6 weeks)  
- Primary goals:
  - Expand total aerobic capacity and discipline frequency.
  - Establish strong technical foundation (especially swim) and economical movement patterns.
- Weekly volume: 8–12 hours (scale by athlete capacity).
- Intensity distribution: Polarized ~85/15 (primary Z1–2 with gentle Z3 touches).
- Key modalities/workouts:
  - Swim (3×/wk): Drills + aerobic; 1 short-threshold/CSS set; focus on body position and catch.
  - Bike (3×/wk): Aerobic endurance; low-cadence torque reps; steady long ride building toward 2–3 hours.
  - Run (3–4×/wk): Easy aerobic runs; strides/hill sprints; 1 steady tempo (20–30 min) every 1–2 weeks.
  - Strength: Continue progressive heavy basics 2×/wk; prioritize form and tendon capacity.
- Transition criteria (into Phase 4):
  - Long ride 2–3 hours and long run 75–90 minutes completed with stable form and manageable RPE.
  - Swim stroke consistency sustained into latter reps of threshold set.
  - No compounding niggles; recovery within 36–48 hours after long sessions.

### Phase 4 — Aerobic Base II (Muscular Endurance & Bricks)
Dates: 2026-02-03 → 2026-03-16 (6 weeks)  
- Primary goals:
  - Build muscular endurance and begin triathlon-specific integration via bricks.
  - Progress long-session durations and introduce more structured tempo/sweet spot.
- Weekly volume: 10–14 hours.
- Intensity distribution: Pyramidal ~75/20/5 (more Z3 work; minimal Z4–5).
- Key modalities/workouts:
  - Swim: 1 CSS set (e.g., 12–20×100 short rest), 1 aerobic pull/paddle, 1 skills/open-water if available.
  - Bike: Sweet spot/upper aerobic intervals (e.g., 3×12–20 min); long ride 3–4 hours every other week with steady blocks.
  - Run: Long run 90–110 minutes; tempo progression (e.g., 2×20–30 min steady); weekly short brick run (15–30 min steady) off the bike.
  - Strength: Shift to 1×/wk maintenance; add light plyometric/elasticity prep 1×/wk if tolerated.
- Transition criteria (into Phase 5):
  - Comfortable with weekly brick; nutrition/hydration tested on long sessions without GI distress.
  - Can hold steady form during longer Z3 blocks across bike and run.

### Phase 5 — Early Race-Specific Build for 70.3
Dates: 2026-03-17 → 2026-04-25 (6 weeks)  
- Primary goals:
  - Consolidate half-ironman–specific durability, pacing, and fueling under fatigue.
  - Rehearse race-day execution (transitions, equipment, and environmental strategy).
- Weekly volume: 11–16 hours (peak of this macrocycle).
- Intensity distribution: Pyramidal trending threshold ~70/25/5 (race-pace focus; limited high-intensity icing).
- Key modalities/workouts:
  - Swim: CSS sets with short rest (e.g., 15–30×100), 1 continuous aerobic endurance swim (2.0–3.0 km), open-water skills/sighting.
  - Bike: Weekly HIM-pace session (e.g., 2×30–45 min within 2.5–3.5 h); over/under sets around threshold; long ride 3.5–4.5 h with extended steady blocks.
  - Run: Weekly brick run 30–60 min at steady/HIM-pace feel off the bike; standalone quality (e.g., 3×2–3 km at HIM pace); long run 90–110 min with the last 20–40 min steady (rotate with brick emphasis to manage load).
  - Strength: Maintain 1×/wk heavy-lite total body; 10–15 min core/hip stability 2×/wk.
  - Heat/humidity prep: Begin 2–3×/wk controlled heat exposures (warm-room sessions or post-workout sauna 15–20 min), strict hydration/electrolytes, monitor post-session recovery.
- Transition criteria (into final taper beyond this plan):
  - Able to complete long brick with stable pacing and acceptable next-day recovery.
  - Fueling plan validated in ≥2 key sessions; no unresolved niggles.

## 4) Microcycle and Load Management

- Loading rhythm: 3 weeks build + 1 week deload across Phases 3–5; Phase 1 uses a descending volume taper; Phase 2 is recovery by design.
- Deload week guidelines: Reduce total volume 25–35%, drop intensity density, keep frequency.
- Key-session density: Typically 2–3 “work” sessions per week total across all disciplines; never stack all quality in adjacent days.
- Recovery anchors: At least one low-load day weekly; mobility on most days; sleep and fueling prioritized after long/brick sessions.

## 5) Phase Transition Gates (Summary)

- Into Phase 2: B race complete; soreness resolves within 3–5 days; no new injuries.
- Into Phase 3: Easy back-to-back aerobic days tolerated; foundational strength patterns pain-free.
- Into Phase 4: Long ride 2–3 h and long run 75–90 min executed without form breakdown; swim stroke stable in late reps.
- Into Phase 5: Weekly brick tolerated; fueling/hydration tested; tempo/sweet spot sessions completed with consistent pacing.
- Into Taper (starts 2026-04-26): Long brick completed cleanly; athlete presents no escalating aches; readiness indicators steady.

## 6) Notes for Downstream Planners

- Pacing language: “HIM pace” refers to sustainably hard, sub-threshold steady state appropriate for ~2.5–3 h on the bike and ~90 min on the run; translate to athlete-specific zones using your preferred model.
- Heat strategy: Begin acclimation in Phase 5; if available, include one weekly session mimicking Danang timing (time of day, temperature).
- Nutrition: Progressive carbohydrate intake practice during long rides/runs from Phase 4 onward; finalize race nutrition in Phase 5.
- Equipment: Phase 5 is the rehearsal window for race wheels/tires, wetsuit or swimskin (as applicable), and transition flow. No major equipment changes after taper starts.
- Contingency: If post-B recovery lingers, extend Phase 2 by 1 week and compress Phase 3 by 1 week (maintain total load integrity rather than forcing intensity).
